- The Settings screen's engine section shows the settings of the scoped project's engine, named after it
  (this machine's under All projects). The Engines screen asks this machine's engine and each connected known
  engine for its status and credentials: a known engine's card shows its status, its projects are listed under
  its name with their trust, Add project opened from an engine's list adds the project on that engine, its
  folders browsed on that engine's host, and a credential is set on the engine the form chooses and removed
  from the engine listing it. A known engine reached while the Engines screen is shown is asked for its own.
