- The command palette (spec §18.5, §18.3): ⌘K (Ctrl+K elsewhere) opens it over any screen, listing the
  screen's commands from its one command list with their keys, then every project, the scoped run's tickets
  and workers (each opening its worker view) and the three themes. Keyboard first: focus starts in its search
  field, typing keeps the entries holding each word, ↑ and ↓ move the selection round, ⏎ runs it and Esc
  closes it; those keys are the palette's own commands in the same list, which its foot names. A click runs
  an entry too.
