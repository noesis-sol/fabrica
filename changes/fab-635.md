- A verifier's turn that a hold outlasts no longer reads as unanswered. A hold keeps its model call at the proxy's
  permit past Pi's timeout and retries, and its turn ends on the error: the verifier now waits for the lift and its
  session carries on to a verdict, rather than the ticket being set aside as UNFINISHED while the hold is on. A
  verifier that settles with no verdict, or whose call failed with its provider not held, is set aside as before.
